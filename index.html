Featured research / Explained visually

Where do the votes go?

In Ireland's PR-STV elections, votes can transfer between candidates as counting progresses. My dissertation studied patterns in those transfers using historical election data and statistical modelling.

2002-2024Election data studied
41%Correct top transfer destination
14.3%Random-choice baseline
0.0564Reported mean absolute error

How the research worked

01Rebuild the data

Clean historical count records and reconstruct missing structured records for 2002.

02Engineer features

Combine election, constituency and candidate information into modelling inputs.

03Compare models

Evaluate Conditional Logit and Poisson GLM alongside Random Forest and XGBoost.

04Test on a later election

Hold out the 2024 election to assess performance on unseen chronological data.

Top destination prediction

Reported top-choice accuracy compared with a random baseline. This measures transfer destinations, not election winners.

Reported model41%
Random baseline14.3%
Why this matters

Historical election records are messy. The project combines data engineering, interpretable statistical modelling and a chronological evaluation rather than relying only on a random train/test split.
